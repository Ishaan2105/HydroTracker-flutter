# Privacy Policy – Hydro Tracker

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026

Hydro Tracker is developed and maintained by **ThreeStack**. Hydro Tracker is a free, offline-first personal hydration tracking application designed to help users monitor their daily water intake and manage hydration reminders.

We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring that you remain in control of your personal information. Hydro Tracker is designed to operate entirely on your device and does not collect or transmit personal data to our servers.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
Hydro Tracker does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personally identifiable information (PII).

The information you enter or configure within the App may include:
- Weight and hydration-related information
- Daily water intake records
- Water intake goals
- Reminder schedules
- Meal times
- Hydration preferences
- App settings and preferences

All such information is stored **locally on your device** using on-device storage/database technology.

**Hydro Tracker:**
- Does not require you to create an account.
- Does not require an email address, phone number, or password.
- Does not use cloud storage or cloud synchronization.
- Does not operate a remote server or database that receives your personal information.
- Does not transmit your hydration records to us or any third party.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
Hydro Tracker may request certain Android permissions that are necessary to provide its reminder and notification functionality:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** The App may request this permission to display hydration reminders and other notifications that you have configured.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`):** The App may use these permissions, where applicable, to schedule hydration reminders and post-meal notifications at the intended times.
- **Battery Optimization (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`):** The App may request this permission optionally to help ensure that scheduled reminders continue to function on devices where aggressive battery-management systems may stop background processes.
- **Device Restart (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** The App may use this permission to restore and reschedule your previously configured local reminders after your device has been restarted.

These permissions are used **only** to provide the App's core reminder and notification functionality.

**Hydro Tracker does not use these permissions to:**
- Access your contacts
- Track your location
- Collect device identifiers for tracking
- Collect personal information
- Monitor your activity outside the App
- Transmit your information to external servers

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security
All information generated or entered within Hydro Tracker is stored locally on your device.

Because Hydro Tracker does not transmit your hydration data to our servers, we do not have access to your locally stored hydration records.

However, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device. If your device is lost, stolen, reset, or its application data is deleted, locally stored information may be permanently lost.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services, Advertising, and Analytics
Hydro Tracker does **not** integrate:
- Third-party analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Tracking SDKs
- Tracking cookies
- Social media tracking tools
- Cloud databases
- Third-party data collection services

No third party receives your hydration information through Hydro Tracker.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Hydro Tracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children or users of any age.

Since the App is designed to operate offline and does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information collected by Hydro Tracker for us to use, sell, or share.

---

### 6. Data Deletion
Since Hydro Tracker stores information locally on your device and does not maintain a remote account or cloud database, we do not hold a copy of your personal hydration data.

You can delete locally stored App data by using the applicable data-clearing or uninstall options provided by your device's operating system.

Please note that deleting the App's local data may permanently remove your saved hydration records, settings, and reminders.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed about how Hydro Tracker handles information.
